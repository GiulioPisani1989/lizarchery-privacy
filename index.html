Lizarchery PureScore — Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026. App: Lizarchery PureScore (com.lizarchery.purescore), by Lizarchery.

The short version

The app collects nothing. Everything you record stays on your phone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app does not ask for permission to use the network.

What the app stores

For each arrow you shoot, the app saves where it landed on the target, how far that is from the centre, which ring it hit and its score, its place in the end, the target and distance, and the session's date and time. It also saves your choices, such as your bow, units and arrow size. This is written to the app's private storage on your device so your history is there the next time you open it.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you export a file and send it yourself. The app has no server, does not send your scores anywhere, and does not include advertising or analytics software. It does not request the Internet permission.

The Support page has a button that opens your own mail app to write to us. If you press it, the message is handled by the mail app you chose, under that app's own policy. We receive only what you decide to send.

Backup files

In Settings you can export your rounds and setups to a file and import one back. Exporting opens your phone's own share sheet, so the file goes only where you choose (for example your drive, an email or another phone). The app itself sends nothing. Importing reads a file you pick and asks what to do if a round exists in both places.

Permissions

None. The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data lives only on your phone. You can delete all sessions in Settings, delete a single session from its page, or remove the app, which erases everything with it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to erase on request.

Children

The app is open to everyone, including children. It collects no personal information from anyone, and it shows no advertising and contains no links to purchases or to the Internet.

We are committed to complying with the Google Play Families Policy. If the app's audience or features change in a way that affects children, this page will say so before the change is released.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a feature starts to use the network, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
